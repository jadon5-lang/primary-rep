About 3 minutes
Purpose: This survey explores academic decisions, feedback, expectations, and experiences of uncertainty among adult higher-education students.

Participation: Taking part is voluntary. You can leave before submitting, without recording your answers.

Privacy: Nine numerical responses and a server-recorded timestamp are saved in shared storage. No names, email addresses, or student IDs are requested. Hosting providers may keep technical access logs. Researchers can inspect anonymous individual scores and times.

Pilot: This software is for testing; formal thesis recruitment requires your institution’s approval and approved consent wording.

About this research: The study examines how family expectations, financial circumstances, and evaluation may influence whether students share uncertainty. The triangle shows the balance of three self-reported influences in plan and their total score in height. A high score is not a clinical measure of stress. It is an exploratory visualization, not a psychological diagnosis.
