About 3 minutes
Purpose: This research explores academic experiences, decision-making, feedback, and uncertainty among higher-education students. Participation is voluntary and intended for adults 18 and older. You may stop before submitting without having your answers recorded.

Data: Nine ratings are recorded anonymously, with a submission timestamp. The researcher can inspect individual ratings and submission times. No names, emails, student IDs, or IP addresses are requested by this offline prototype. Once submitted, answers cannot be changed or withdrawn individually because no identity is linked to them.

Testing: This is an offline demonstration, not a university-approved live study. Responses remain on this browser only unless exported.
